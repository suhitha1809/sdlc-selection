Patient Appointment Scheduling Policy - Example Source

The appointment scheduling service shall verify the patient's identity before creating or updating a clinical appointment.
The service shall confirm eligibility for the requested clinic, specialty, or follow-up visit using the approved scheduling rules and clinician availability data.
The system must record the scheduling decision, the clinician assigned, the originating staff member or portal user, and the timestamp in an auditable event log.
Patient health information collected during scheduling must be restricted to authorised clinical roles, protected by role-based access control, and retained according to the approved retention policy.
The portal should notify patients when an appointment is booked, rescheduled, or cancelled and should provide confirmation details, reminder timing, and instructions for rescheduling or cancellation.
The system shall prevent double booking for the same clinician, room, and time slot while preserving continuity for high-priority follow-up visits.
Failed or incomplete appointment requests must be routed to a trained clinic administrator, scheduler, or triage coordinator for review and manual resolution.
The service should provide an appointment availability target approved by the clinical operations owner before production release and should support escalation when clinic capacity is exceeded.
The scheduling workflow must preserve patient confidentiality, support accessibility requirements, and produce a traceable audit record for every approved, changed, or cancelled booking.
